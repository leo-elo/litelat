Dear all,nnWe are pleased to inform you that the dates for the next LitDigBR have already been confirmed for 2026. We are still finalizing the details of the program, but we can already announce that the event will be held in a hybrid format, with both on-site and online activities.nnThe event will include in-person participation of invited speakers, workshops, and paper presentations by researchers in digital literature. In the coming months, we will share further information regarding submissions, registration, and the full program.nnWe kindly ask for your support in disseminating this preliminary announcement among students, colleagues, and others who may be interested in digital literature.nnBest regards,
nn
